Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026  |  App: HydroGlow  |  Package: com.tsw.hydroglow

HydroGlow ("we," "our," or "us") provides a hydration logging and reminder app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, where it stays, and when the internet is used.

1. Summary

  • Local first: Drink logs, daily goals, check-in streaks, reminder schedules, and drink modes are stored only on your device.
  • No ads: We do not show advertisements and do not integrate advertising SDKs (such as AdMob).
  • No cloud account: We do not operate our own servers to collect or store your hydration data, and we do not upload your logs to the cloud.
  • Limited network use: The App works offline for core features. Network access is used for Google Play purchases and purchase restoration, and when you choose to open this Privacy Policy or our Terms of Service inside the App.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Depending on how you use HydroGlow, the following may be saved locally on your phone or tablet:

  • Daily water intake logs and counts
  • Daily goal amount and drink mode settings (including custom modes)
  • Check-in history and streak information
  • Reminder times and alert preferences (sound, vibration, background reminder option)
  • Membership (Pro) status cached locally after a successful Google Play purchase
  • Your choice to accept this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service

We do not require your name, email, or a user account to use the App.

3. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers in the App.
  • We do not connect to Apple Health, Google Health Connect, or similar health platforms in the current version.
  • We do not sync your hydration data across devices via our own backend.

4. Permissions and Device Features

To provide reminders and optional Pro features, the App may ask for:

  • Notifications — to show hydration reminders
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) — to schedule timely local alerts where supported by your device
  • Vibration — if you enable vibration in reminder settings
  • Foreground service — only if you turn on optional background reminders to improve delivery on some devices
  • Boot completed — to reschedule local alarms after device restart

You can change or revoke permissions in your device system settings at any time.

5. Purchases (Google Play)

If you subscribe or buy a lifetime Pro option, payment and receipt validation are handled by Google Play Billing. Google may process payment information according to Google's Privacy Policy. We receive purchase status from Google to unlock Pro features on your device; we do not receive your full payment card details.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your hydration data remains on your device until you clear App storage, uninstall the App, or reset the device. Because we do not host your data, uninstalling HydroGlow typically removes locally stored logs and settings. For questions about deleting data, contact us (see below).

7. Children's Privacy

HydroGlow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. International Users

Data described above stays on your device. When you use Google Play, Google's services may process data according to their policies and your region.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated Policy.
