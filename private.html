Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 12, 2026  |  App Name: HydroGlow: Water Reminder

HydroGlow: Water Reminder ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with a seamless hydration tracking experience, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Data You Provide: Information you voluntarily provide when setting up your profile, such as your daily water intake goals, weight, activity level, and notification preferences.
  • Health & Fitness Data: With your explicit permission, the App may integrate with platform health kits (such as Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect) to read and sync water consumption data.
  • Automatically Collected Information: Non-personal diagnostic data, app crash logs, and usage statistics to help us improve app performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To calculate personalized daily water intake recommendations.
  • To deliver timely hydration reminders and track your daily/monthly check-in streaks.
  • To sync your progress securely across your devices.
  • To analyze app performance and fix technical bugs.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your personal health and hydration data are primarily stored locally on your device or secured via encrypted cloud infrastructure. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identifiable information to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party analytics and subscription management tools (such as RevenueCat for in-app purchases) that comply with strict data protection standards.

5. Your Choices & Rights

You can manage or revoke permissions (such as notifications or Health data access) at any time through your device system settings. You may also request the deletion of your account and associated data by contacting our support.

6. Children’s Privacy

HydroGlow: Water Reminder is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.
